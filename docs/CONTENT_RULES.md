# Content rules

## Single sources of truth

- `content/business.json`: phone, cities, addresses, hours, warranty, form endpoint, analytics.
- `content/pages.json`: route content, SEO, symptoms, services, prices, FAQ and Figma mapping.
- `content/navigation.json`: global navigation.

## Required confirmation before production

- Exact Moscow and Saint Petersburg addresses.
- Whether one phone is valid for both cities.
- WhatsApp and Telegram links.
- Form delivery endpoint.
- Legally correct warranty wording.
- Real price ranges.
- Legal entity, privacy policy and personal data consent.

## Claims

- Do not say an image is a specific transmission model unless technically verified.
- Mark unconfirmed prices as approximate.
- Do not use competitors' images without a licence or explicit permission.
- Do not promise repair time or warranty outside approved business data.
